## The Rex Method (in one paragraph)

Every video maps to one of three buckets: **Resources** (a tool that earns a spot on my machine), **Experiments** (a weekend build that may or may not work), **eXecution** (the real, shipped version after the experiment survives). If something doesn't fit one of those three, I don't film it.

## What this channel covers

- Local AI: Ollama, LM Studio, MLX, llama.cpp, quantization tradeoffs
- AI agents: Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, Windsurf — real projects, not demos
- Hardware: the M-series Mac, consumer GPUs, edge devices
- CLIs that make agents useful: Vercel, GitHub, Supabase, AWS, Google Workspace

## What this channel doesn't cover
