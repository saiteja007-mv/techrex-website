# OpenClaw Review: 80% of My Apps Replaced After 3 Months

Three months ago, I started using **OpenClaw** as my personal AI assistant. The creator claimed "80% of apps will be dead" — I was skeptical. Here's what happened. 🦖

## 📱 The Apps I Used to Need

Before OpenClaw, my daily workflow required:
1. **Calendar app** (Google Calendar)
2. **Notes app** (Notion + Apple Notes)
3. **Task manager** (Todoist)
4. **Email client** (Gmail)
5. **File manager** (Dropbox + Google Drive)
6. **Calculator** (Calculator.app)
7. **Weather app** (Weather Channel)
8. **Timer/Alarm** (Clock.app)
9. **Calculator** (various)
10. **Browser bookmarks** (Chrome)

## 🤖 How OpenClaw Replaced Them

### 1. Calendar & Scheduling
**Old way:** Open Google Calendar, manually check availability, create events
**OpenClaw:** "Schedule a meeting with John next Tuesday at 2 PM"

### 2. Notes & Documentation
**Old way:** Open Notion, create page, format content
**OpenClaw:** "Take a note about the LLMfit project ideas and save it"

### 3. Task Management
**Old way:** Open Todoist, add task, set due date, assign labels
**OpenClaw:** "Remind me to deploy the website tomorrow at 9 AM"

### 4. File Search
**Old way:** Open Finder, navigate folders, search
**OpenClaw:** "Find the video thumbnail I downloaded last week"

### 5. Quick Calculations
**Old way:** Open Calculator, type numbers
**OpenClaw:** "What's 847 divided by 13, and convert to percentage"

## ⚡ The Game-Changer: Context Awareness

What makes OpenClaw different from other AI assistants is **context**. It knows:
- What's on your screen
- Your recent files
- Your upcoming calendar
- Your preferences

## ⚖️ The Honest Truth

### What OpenClaw Does Exceptionally Well
- Natural language commands
- Context-aware responses
- Multi-step task automation
- Quick information retrieval

### What Still Needs Work
- Complex creative tasks (still need dedicated apps)
- Heavy file editing (still need VS Code, Photoshop, etc.)
- Offline functionality

## 🚀 Who Should Use OpenClaw?

**Perfect for:**
- Knowledge workers
- Developers
- Content creators
- Anyone who values efficiency

**Maybe skip if:**
- You prefer manual control
- You work in highly regulated industries
- You need 100% offline capability
