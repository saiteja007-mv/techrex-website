## Tech Stack Recommendation

| Option | Best For | Complexity | Cost |
|--------|----------|------------|------|
| **Astro + Vercel** | Static, fast, SEO-friendly | Low-Medium | Free tier |
| **Next.js + Vercel** | Full control, dynamic content | Medium | Free tier |
| **Framer** | Visual design, quick launch | Low | Paid |
| **Webflow** | No-code, design flexibility | Low | Paid |

**Recommended:** **Astro + Vercel**
- Perfect for content-heavy sites
- Fast performance
- Great YouTube embed support
- Easy to maintain
- Excellent SEO out of the box
