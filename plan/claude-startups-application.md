## Registering the business (optional for this program)

The program doesn't list incorporation as a requirement ("founded in the last 5 years"), so
you can apply now as a solo founder. Registering still helps credibility and later programs.

1. **Pick an entity:** a single-member LLC in your home state is the usual first step for a
   solo founder. State filing fees vary (roughly $50 to $500).
2. **Get an EIN:** free, online, from the IRS (irs.gov), usually issued same day.
3. **Open a business bank account** with the LLC papers + EIN.
4. **Visa check first:** if you're on a work or student visa (F-1/OPT, H-1B, etc.), ask your
   DSO or an immigration attorney before you register or operate a business. Owning an LLC and
   working for it are treated differently depending on status.

After registering, update the "Legal entity" field and resubmit or note it in the application.
